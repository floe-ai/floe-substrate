Ordinary publish/subscribe on a [Context](context.md) for non-graph
communication.

## Legacy

Subscriptions also index old Scope graphs, which routed by subscription. A
canonical ScopeCompositionRevision never dispatches by subscription, and one
revision never mixes subscription routing with Edge routing. Old graphs may be
imported and inspected; new or republished compositions use Edges only.
